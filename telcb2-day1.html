01

Introduction

Today we look at Schriftlicher Ausdruck. This is the writing part of the written exam.

You write one letter or email. It is a bit formal — like a نامه اداری, not a message to a friend. You read a short advertisement (Anzeige). Then you write. You get two tasks. You choose one. Almost always, one task is a request for information (Anfrage / Bitte um Information). The other is a complaint (Beschwerde).

Why this part is important

Writing is 45 points from 300. That is 15% of the exam. It looks small. Still, it is important for two reasons:

  • The written block must reach 60% by itself. You need 135 points from 225 (reading, language, listening, writing). A good speaking score cannot save a weak written score. Writing is a safe place to take points.
  • You can practise this part at home. Reading and listening give you new texts. In writing, you already know the shape of the letter. About 60% of a good letter is a fixed plan. You can learn that plan before the exam.

Simple picture: reading and listening test your German. Writing tests if you have a plan. People who lose points here usually lose them on structure and formality — not on grammar.

02

What you will learn today

At the end of this lesson you will be able to:

  1. Explain the task, and how the 45 points are given.
  2. Choose task A or task B in under two minutes.
  3. Write a fixed six-part letter. It works for both types.
  4. Use about 40 ready German phrases. They fit many topics.
  5. Write the Leitpunkte (content points) the way the examiners want.
  6. Use the 30 minutes well, so you do not run out of time.

Today’s goal, in one sentence

Leave with a letter plan you can use for any topic. Also know what the two examiners look at.

The main lesson

The facts

Time
30minutes
Length
150+words minimum
Points
45of 300 total
Tasks
1 of 2you choose

You get a short advertisement and two tasks. Each task has four Leitpunkte (content points). You must write about three of them. Or: two of them, plus one extra point of your own. There is no official maximum. 150–200 words is a good length. Longer letters often add more mistakes.

The two task types

TypeWhat you're doing
Anfrage / Bitte um Information
Request for information		 You saw an ad (language course, trip, gym, event, apartment). You want more details. You explain your situation. You ask clear questions. You ask them to reply.
Beschwerde
Complaint		 You booked or bought something. It was not like the advertisement. You describe the problem. You explain what happened because of it. You ask for a clear solution.

Topics that come back again and again

The exam does not publish a topic list. Still, schools and past candidates see the same situations again and again. Learn words for these:

  • Hotel / accommodation — dirty room, noise, missing breakfast, no air conditioning, wrong room category
  • Online orders — wrong size or colour, damaged goods, late delivery, missing parts (bicycle, vacuum cleaner, coffee machine, furniture)
  • Courses — language course, cooking course, computer course: group too large, teacher changed, course cancelled
  • Travel — trip did not match the brochure, excursions cancelled, bad organisation
  • Fitness studio / swimming pool / sauna — broken equipment, overcrowding, unclear contract
  • Restaurant / event — poor service, cancelled concert or festival

See the pattern: the ad promised X, but real life was Y. Every complaint letter is this one idea, written longer.

How you are scored

Two examiners read your letter. They score three things. Each thing gets A / B / C / D. The points are added (maximum 15). Then they multiply by 3. So the total is 45.

What they scoreWhat it really means
Berücksichtigung der Leitpunkte
Did you do the task?		 Did you write about three content points? And did you write more than one short sentence for each point? One sentence per point is weak. Two or three sentences is good.
Kommunikative Gestaltung
Does it look like a real letter?		 Does it look and sound like a real letter? Subject line, greeting, clear paragraphs, connecting words, formal language, a proper ending.
Formale Richtigkeit
Grammar and spelling		 Grammar, words, spelling. You do not need a perfect text. The important question: do mistakes stop the reader from understanding? If the letter is easy to read the first time, you can still score well with small mistakes.

The most important result: two of the three scores are not about grammar. Structure and the task are two-thirds of the points. You can prepare both at home.

The six parts of the letter

Use this same plan for both task types. Only the middle part changes.

1
Betreff — subject lineShort and clear. Say what and when. Never skip it.
2
Anrede — greetingSehr geehrte Damen und Herren, then a comma. Start the next line with a small letter.
3
Einleitung — openingOne or two sentences: who you are, what you booked or saw, and why you write.
4
Hauptteil — the bodyYour three Leitpunkte. One short paragraph for each. Use a clear order.
5
Forderung / Bitte — what you wantWhat should they do? By when? This makes the letter feel finished.
6
Schlussformel — endingMit freundlichen Grüßen + your name. No comma after it.

A model complaint letter

Read this letter. See how simple the plan is. The topic is invented. The plan is always the same.

Betreff Beschwerde über die Sprachreise nach Valencia vom 3. bis 17. Juli
Anrede

Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren,

Einleitung — reason for writing

vor drei Wochen habe ich über Ihr Reisebüro eine zweiwöchige Sprachreise nach Valencia gebucht. Leider muss ich Ihnen mitteilen, dass ich mit dem Verlauf der Reise überhaupt nicht zufrieden war.

Leitpunkt 1 + 2 — the problems

In Ihrer Anzeige haben Sie einen Intensivkurs mit höchstens acht Teilnehmern versprochen. Tatsächlich saßen wir jedoch zu achtzehnt in einem Raum, sodass der Lehrer kaum auf einzelne Fragen eingehen konnte. Darüber hinaus lag die Unterkunft nicht, wie angekündigt, in der Nähe der Schule, sondern etwa eine Stunde mit dem Bus entfernt.

Leitpunkt 3 — the consequence

Dadurch habe ich jeden Tag wertvolle Lernzeit verloren. Der eigentliche Zweck meiner Reise, nämlich mein Spanisch für den Beruf zu verbessern, wurde auf diese Weise nicht erreicht. Sie können sich sicher vorstellen, wie enttäuscht ich war.

Forderung — the demand

Aus diesem Grund bitte ich Sie, mir die Hälfte des Reisepreises zurückzuerstatten. Ich wäre Ihnen sehr dankbar, wenn Sie mir bis zum 30. September schriftlich antworten würden.

Schlussformel

Mit freundlichen Grüßen
Anna Kowalska

That is about 175 words. Only six sentences are real content. The rest is a ready formula.

Grammar that examiners like (and is easy)

You do not need hard German. You need three or four structures, used correctly:

  • Konjunktiv II for politeness — Ich wäre Ihnen dankbar, wenn … / Könnten Sie mir bitte mitteilen, ob …
  • Passive for objectivity — Das Zimmer wurde nicht gereinigt. (The room was not cleaned.)
  • Indirect questions — Ich möchte wissen, ob / wie viel / wann …
  • Connectors beyond und/aber — außerdem, darüber hinaus, allerdings, jedoch, deshalb, aus diesem Grund, dadurch, obwohl
04

Ready-made phrases (Redemittel)

Learn these as small pieces. Do not learn one whole letter by heart. Examiners can see a memorised letter. But they cannot see that you learned your opening at home — because every real letter has an opening.

Opening — why you are writing
Anfrage
mit großem Interesse habe ich Ihre Anzeige in der Zeitung gelesen.
I read your advertisement in the newspaper with great interest.
ich schreibe Ihnen, weil ich einige Fragen zu Ihrem Angebot habe.
I'm writing because I have some questions about your offer.
Beschwerde
vor zwei Wochen habe ich bei Ihnen … gebucht / bestellt.
Two weeks ago I booked / ordered … with you.
leider muss ich Ihnen mitteilen, dass ich mit … sehr unzufrieden bin.
Unfortunately I have to inform you that I'm very dissatisfied with …
Describing the problem
In Ihrer Anzeige haben Sie … versprochen. Tatsächlich war es jedoch anders.
In your advertisement you promised … In reality, however, it was different.
Das Zimmer entsprach überhaupt nicht der Beschreibung.
The room did not match the description at all.
Darüber hinaus funktionierte die Heizung während des gesamten Aufenthalts nicht.
Furthermore, the heating did not work during the entire stay.
Statt der bestellten Größe M habe ich Größe S erhalten.
Instead of the size M I ordered, I received size S.
Explaining the result — many people skip this
Dadurch habe ich viel Zeit und Geld verloren.
As a result, I lost a lot of time and money.
Sie können sich sicher vorstellen, wie enttäuscht ich war.
You can surely imagine how disappointed I was.
Aus diesem Grund konnte ich den Kurs nicht wie geplant besuchen.
For this reason I could not attend the course as planned.
Asking questions (Anfrage)
Könnten Sie mir bitte mitteilen, wie viel der Kurs insgesamt kostet?
Could you please tell me how much the course costs in total?
Außerdem würde mich interessieren, ob eine Unterkunft im Preis enthalten ist.
I would also be interested to know whether accommodation is included in the price.
Wäre es möglich, den Termin später zu verschieben?
Would it be possible to move the appointment to a later date?
Eine weitere Frage betrifft die Gruppengröße.
A further question concerns the group size.
Your demand / request
Deshalb bitte ich Sie, mir den vollen Betrag zurückzuerstatten.
I therefore ask you to refund me the full amount.
Ich erwarte von Ihnen eine angemessene Entschädigung.
I expect appropriate compensation from you.
Ich wäre Ihnen dankbar, wenn Sie mir bis zum 15. Mai antworten würden.
I would be grateful if you replied to me by 15 May.
Für eine schnelle Rückmeldung wäre ich Ihnen sehr dankbar.
I would be very grateful for a quick response.
Closing
Vielen Dank im Voraus für Ihre Bemühungen.
Thank you in advance for your efforts.
Ich freue mich auf Ihre Antwort.
I look forward to your reply.
Mit freundlichen Grüßen
Yours sincerely — the standard formal closing. No comma after it.
Connectors — put these in the body

außerdem (besides) · darüber hinaus (furthermore) · allerdings (however) · jedoch (however) · deshalb / aus diesem Grund (therefore) · dadurch (as a result) · obwohl (although) · statt (instead of) · zunächst (first of all) · schließlich (finally)

05

Exam tips

Time management — 30 minutes

0–3
Read and chooseRead the ad and both tasks. Choose the one where you know more words. Choose fast. Do not change your mind.
3–7
PlanMark the three Leitpunkte you will use. Write 2–3 keywords next to each. Do not write full sentences yet.
7–25
WriteWrite from start to end. Follow the six parts. Do not stop to make it pretty.
25–30
CheckVerb position, capital letters on nouns, Sie forms, and that the greeting and ending are both there.

What the examiners want

  • Three content points. Each point has more than one sentence.
  • A text that is easy to understand the first time
  • Formal language all the way — Sie from the first word to the last
  • Clear letter parts: subject, greeting, paragraphs, ending
  • Different connecting words and different sentence starts

Common mistakes to avoid

No Betreff, or a vague one like „Problem“.
Beschwerde über die Buchung Nr. 4517 vom 12. März — concrete and dated.
Starting with Hallo or Liebe Firma.
Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren, — the safe default when you don't know the name.
Slipping into du halfway through.
Capital Sie / Ihnen / Ihr throughout. Check this specifically in your final read.
Writing an angry complaint: Das ist eine Katastrophe!
Stay calm and clear: problem → result → what you want. Polite language gets points. Strong emotion does not.
Writing all four Leitpunkte with only one sentence each.
Three points. Two or three sentences each. Better to go deep than to cover everything in a thin way.
Writing a whole memorised letter that does not fit the task.
Learn the frame (opening, connectors, ending). Write the content new each time.
Writing 300 words to look strong.
150–200 words. Every extra sentence is another chance for a mistake.

Three small practical tips

  • Count words once. Count the words in three handwritten lines. Take the average. Multiply by the number of lines. Do not count word by word.
  • Leave an empty line between paragraphs. A clear look is part of Kommunikative Gestaltung. It costs you nothing.
  • If you take the computer version, practise typing German on the same keyboard — including ä ö ü ß. Handwriting and typing are not the same speed.
06

Short review

The format

  • One slightly formal letter about an ad. Choose 1 of 2 tasks
  • Usually Anfrage (request) vs Beschwerde (complaint)
  • Four Leitpunkte are given — write about three, in a full way
  • Minimum 150 words · 30 minutes · 45 points

The scoring

  • Berücksichtigung der Leitpunkte — did you cover the points, with enough detail
  • Kommunikative Gestaltung — does it look and sound like a letter
  • Formale Richtigkeit — do mistakes stop the reader from understanding
  • Two of three scores are about structure, not grammar

The plan to remember

  • Betreff → Anrede → Einleitung → Hauptteil (3 points) → Forderung → Schlussformel

The most useful habit

  • Use the first 7 minutes to choose and plan. People who start writing at once often run out of time and forget the ending.

Before Day 2

If you want to practise: write one complaint letter about a hotel, and one request letter about a language course. Both in 30 minutes. Both 150–200 words. Use the six-part plan exactly as it is here.
