01

Introduction

Today we cover the Schriftlicher Ausdruck — the writing section of the written exam.

You write one semi-formal letter or e-mail in reaction to a short advertisement (Anzeige). You get two prompts and choose one. Almost always, one is a request for information (Anfrage / Bitte um Information) and the other is a complaint (Beschwerde).

Why this part matters

Writing is worth 45 of the 300 total points — 15% of the exam. That sounds small, but two things make it disproportionately important:

  • The written block must reach 60% on its own. You need 135 of 225 points across reading, language elements, listening and writing. The blocks do not compensate each other. Writing is where you can most reliably grab points.
  • It is the only part you can fully rehearse. Reading and listening throw unknown texts at you. In writing, you already know the structure of the answer before you walk in. About 60% of a good letter is a skeleton you can prepare in advance.

Think of it this way: reading and listening test your German. Writing tests whether you have prepared. Candidates who lose points here almost always lose them on structure and formality — not on grammar.

What we're going to learn today

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

  1. Describe the exact task format and know how the 45 points are awarded.
  2. Decide between task A and task B in under two minutes.
  3. Write a fixed six-block structure that works for both letter types.
  4. Use around 40 ready-made German phrases that fit any topic.
  5. Handle the Leitpunkte (content points) the way the raters expect.
  6. Manage the 30 minutes without running out of time.

Today's goal in one sentence

03

The main lesson

The hard facts

Time
30minutes
Length
150+words minimum
Points
45of 300 total
Tasks
1 of 2you choose

You are given a short advertisement plus two task options. Each option lists four Leitpunkte (content points). You must deal with three of them — or two of them plus one relevant point of your own. There is no official maximum length, but 150–200 words is the sweet spot. Longer texts mostly add errors.

The two task types

TypeWhat you're doing
Anfrage / Bitte um Information
Request for information		 You saw an ad (language course, trip, fitness studio, event, apartment) and you want details. You explain your situation, ask precise questions, and request a reply.
Beschwerde
Complaint		 You booked or bought something and it did not match the advertisement. You describe the problem, explain the consequence, and demand a concrete solution.

Topics that come back again and again

The exam does not publish a topic list, but prep schools and past candidates report the same scenarios repeatedly. Prepare vocabulary for these:

  • Hotel / accommodation — dirty room, noise, missing breakfast, no air conditioning, wrong room category
  • Online orders — wrong size or colour, damaged goods, late delivery, missing parts (bicycle, vacuum cleaner, coffee machine, furniture)
  • Courses — language course, cooking course, computer course: group too large, teacher changed, course cancelled
  • Travel — trip did not match the brochure, excursions cancelled, bad organisation
  • Fitness studio / swimming pool / sauna — broken equipment, overcrowding, unclear contract
  • Restaurant / event — poor service, cancelled concert or festival

Notice the pattern: advertisement promised X, reality was Y. Every complaint letter is that one sentence, expanded.

How you are scored

Two licensed raters grade your text on three criteria. Each is graded A / B / C / D, the points are added (maximum 15), then multiplied by 3 to reach 45.

CriterionWhat it actually means
Berücksichtigung der Leitpunkte
Task fulfilment		 Did you handle three content points, and did you give each one more than a single clause? One sentence per point is a weak answer. Two to three sentences is a good one.
Kommunikative Gestaltung
Communicative design		 Does it look and sound like a real letter? Subject line, correct salutation, logical paragraphs, connectors, consistent formal register, proper closing formula.
Formale Richtigkeit
Formal accuracy		 Grammar, vocabulary, spelling. The key threshold is not perfection — it is whether errors block understanding. A text that reads smoothly on first reading scores well even with small mistakes.

The most important consequence: two of the three criteria have nothing to do with your grammar level. Structure and task handling are worth two-thirds of the score, and both are fully controllable in advance.

The six-block skeleton

Use this same architecture for both task types. Only the middle changes.

1
Betreff — subject lineShort, concrete, includes what and when. Never skip it.
2
Anrede — salutationSehr geehrte Damen und Herren, then a comma, then start the next line with a lowercase letter.
3
Einleitung — openingOne or two sentences: who you are, what you booked/saw, and why you are writing.
4
Hauptteil — bodyYour three Leitpunkte, one short paragraph each, in a logical order.
5
Forderung / Bitte — your requestWhat you want them to do, and by when. This is what makes the letter feel finished.
6
Schlussformel — closingMit freundlichen Grüßen + your name. No comma after it.

A model complaint letter

Read this and notice how mechanical it is. The topic is invented; the architecture is fixed.

Betreff Beschwerde über die Sprachreise nach Valencia vom 3. bis 17. Juli
Anrede

Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren,

Einleitung — reason for writing

vor drei Wochen habe ich über Ihr Reisebüro eine zweiwöchige Sprachreise nach Valencia gebucht. Leider muss ich Ihnen mitteilen, dass ich mit dem Verlauf der Reise überhaupt nicht zufrieden war.

Leitpunkt 1 + 2 — the problems

In Ihrer Anzeige haben Sie einen Intensivkurs mit höchstens acht Teilnehmern versprochen. Tatsächlich saßen wir jedoch zu achtzehnt in einem Raum, sodass der Lehrer kaum auf einzelne Fragen eingehen konnte. Darüber hinaus lag die Unterkunft nicht, wie angekündigt, in der Nähe der Schule, sondern etwa eine Stunde mit dem Bus entfernt.

Leitpunkt 3 — the consequence

Dadurch habe ich jeden Tag wertvolle Lernzeit verloren. Der eigentliche Zweck meiner Reise, nämlich mein Spanisch für den Beruf zu verbessern, wurde auf diese Weise nicht erreicht. Sie können sich sicher vorstellen, wie enttäuscht ich war.

Forderung — the demand

Aus diesem Grund bitte ich Sie, mir die Hälfte des Reisepreises zurückzuerstatten. Ich wäre Ihnen sehr dankbar, wenn Sie mir bis zum 30. September schriftlich antworten würden.

Schlussformel

Mit freundlichen Grüßen
Anna Kowalska

That is roughly 175 words and took six sentences of real content. Everything else was formula.

Grammar that impresses raters (and is easy)

You do not need complicated German. You need three or four structures used correctly:

  • Konjunktiv II for politeness — Ich wäre Ihnen dankbar, wenn … / Könnten Sie mir bitte mitteilen, ob …
  • Passive for objectivity — Das Zimmer wurde nicht gereinigt. (The room was not cleaned.)
  • Indirect questions — Ich möchte wissen, ob / wie viel / wann …
  • Connectors beyond und/aber — außerdem, darüber hinaus, allerdings, jedoch, deshalb, aus diesem Grund, dadurch, obwohl
04

Ready-made phrases (Redemittel)

Learn these as building blocks, not as a whole memorised letter. Raters can tell when a text was learned by heart — but nobody can tell that you knew your opening formula in advance, because every real letter uses one.

Opening — why you are writing
Anfrage
mit großem Interesse habe ich Ihre Anzeige in der Zeitung gelesen.
I read your advertisement in the newspaper with great interest.
ich schreibe Ihnen, weil ich einige Fragen zu Ihrem Angebot habe.
I'm writing because I have some questions about your offer.
Beschwerde
vor zwei Wochen habe ich bei Ihnen … gebucht / bestellt.
Two weeks ago I booked / ordered … with you.
leider muss ich Ihnen mitteilen, dass ich mit … sehr unzufrieden bin.
Unfortunately I have to inform you that I'm very dissatisfied with …
Describing the problem
In Ihrer Anzeige haben Sie … versprochen. Tatsächlich war es jedoch anders.
In your advertisement you promised … In reality, however, it was different.
Das Zimmer entsprach überhaupt nicht der Beschreibung.
The room did not match the description at all.
Darüber hinaus funktionierte die Heizung während des gesamten Aufenthalts nicht.
Furthermore, the heating did not work during the entire stay.
Statt der bestellten Größe M habe ich Größe S erhalten.
Instead of the size M I ordered, I received size S.
Explaining the consequence — the point most candidates skip
Dadurch habe ich viel Zeit und Geld verloren.
As a result, I lost a lot of time and money.
Sie können sich sicher vorstellen, wie enttäuscht ich war.
You can surely imagine how disappointed I was.
Aus diesem Grund konnte ich den Kurs nicht wie geplant besuchen.
For this reason I could not attend the course as planned.
Asking questions (Anfrage)
Könnten Sie mir bitte mitteilen, wie viel der Kurs insgesamt kostet?
Could you please tell me how much the course costs in total?
Außerdem würde mich interessieren, ob eine Unterkunft im Preis enthalten ist.
I would also be interested to know whether accommodation is included in the price.
Wäre es möglich, den Termin später zu verschieben?
Would it be possible to move the appointment to a later date?
Eine weitere Frage betrifft die Gruppengröße.
A further question concerns the group size.
Your demand / request
Deshalb bitte ich Sie, mir den vollen Betrag zurückzuerstatten.
I therefore ask you to refund me the full amount.
Ich erwarte von Ihnen eine angemessene Entschädigung.
I expect appropriate compensation from you.
Ich wäre Ihnen dankbar, wenn Sie mir bis zum 15. Mai antworten würden.
I would be grateful if you replied to me by 15 May.
Für eine schnelle Rückmeldung wäre ich Ihnen sehr dankbar.
I would be very grateful for a quick response.
Closing
Vielen Dank im Voraus für Ihre Bemühungen.
Thank you in advance for your efforts.
Ich freue mich auf Ihre Antwort.
I look forward to your reply.
Mit freundlichen Grüßen
Yours sincerely — the standard formal closing. No comma after it.
Connectors — sprinkle these through the body

außerdem (besides) · darüber hinaus (furthermore) · allerdings (however) · jedoch (however) · deshalb / aus diesem Grund (therefore) · dadurch (as a result) · obwohl (although) · statt (instead of) · zunächst (first of all) · schließlich (finally)

05

Exam tips

Time management — 30 minutes

0–3
Read and chooseRead the ad and both tasks. Pick the one whose vocabulary you know better. Decide fast and never look back.
3–7
PlanMark the three Leitpunkte you'll use. Write 2–3 keywords next to each. Do not write full sentences yet.
7–25
WriteStraight through, following the six-block skeleton. Don't stop to polish.
25–30
CheckVerb position, capital letters on nouns, Sie forms, and that the salutation and closing are both there.

What the raters are looking for

  • Three content points, each developed with more than one clause
  • A text that is understandable on the first reading
  • Consistent formal register — Sie from first word to last
  • Visible letter features: subject, salutation, paragraphs, closing
  • Variety in connectors and sentence openings

Common mistakes to avoid

No Betreff, or a vague one like „Problem“.
Beschwerde über die Buchung Nr. 4517 vom 12. März — concrete and dated.
Starting with Hallo or Liebe Firma.
Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren, — the safe default when you don't know the name.
Slipping into du halfway through.
Capital Sie / Ihnen / Ihr throughout. Check this specifically in your final read.
Writing an angry complaint: Das ist eine Katastrophe!
Cool, factual, solution-focused: problem → consequence → demand. Politeness scores; emotion doesn't.
Squeezing all four Leitpunkte in with one sentence each.
Three points, two to three sentences each. Depth over coverage.
Reciting a whole memorised letter that barely fits the prompt.
Memorise the frame (opening, connectors, closing). Write the content fresh.
Writing 300 words to look impressive.
150–200 words. Every extra sentence is another chance to make an error.

Three quick practical tips

  • Count words once. Count the words in three of your handwritten lines, take the average, multiply by your number of lines. Don't count word by word.
  • Leave a blank line between paragraphs. Visual structure is part of Kommunikative Gestaltung and costs nothing.
  • If you're taking the digital version, practise typing German on the keyboard layout you'll actually have — including ä ö ü ß. Writing by hand and typing are not the same speed.
06

Recap

The format

  • One semi-formal letter responding to an ad; choose 1 of 2 tasks
  • Usually Anfrage (request) vs Beschwerde (complaint)
  • Four Leitpunkte given — handle three, properly
  • Minimum 150 words · 30 minutes · 45 points

The scoring

  • Berücksichtigung der Leitpunkte — did you cover the points, in depth
  • Kommunikative Gestaltung — does it look and sound like a letter
  • Formale Richtigkeit — do errors block understanding
  • Two of three criteria are about structure, not grammar

The skeleton to remember

  • Betreff → Anrede → Einleitung → Hauptteil (3 points) → Forderung → Schlussformel

The single most useful habit

  • Spend the first 7 minutes choosing and planning. Candidates who start writing immediately are the ones who run out of time and forget the closing.
